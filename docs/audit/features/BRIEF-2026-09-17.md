## 1. The single worst thing I found

**A guest 16+ minutes late loses her table, her cashback and her reliability score in one silent
cron tick, can't be seated when she walks in, and is then told the booking was «لغوشده».**

Measured, not inferred. These are the production cron functions on a fresh Postgres 17 (F001 §1.1, raw
output):
`confirmed→running_late→no_show` both by `cron` in one run · **0** SMS before · one `booking_noshow` SMS
after · cashback −40 · `strikeCount 1` · reliability **75→0** for a first-time user ·
`canTransition(no_show→seated) = false`.

The app has no «دیرتر می‌رسم», no restaurant phone (the `Restaurant` model has no phone field), no chat
from the booking, and the restaurant can't change the 15-minute grace (0 writers). The app *does* warn
before a late cancel, which costs less than this.
