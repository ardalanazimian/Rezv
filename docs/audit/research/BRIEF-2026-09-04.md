## What changed in the market this batch

- **OpenTable tightened restaurant lock-in** (April 2026 "system of record" clause) at the same
  moment its two biggest rivals bought scale via M&A — Amex merging Tock into Resy (Feb 2026,
  doubling Resy's inventory to ~25k+ venues) and DoorDash completing its ~$1.2B acquisition of
  SevenRooms (June 2025). Read together: the global market's three biggest players are all
  consolidating or tightening terms in the same 18-month window. Iran's market is nowhere near this
  stage (see below) — but the fee-opacity and lock-in complaints are exactly what to design against
  as Rezervno's own commercial terms mature.
- **Quandoo — a live shutdown.** The one pure per-cover-commission player in our global set is
  winding down completely by end of 2026, in a market that shifted toward flat-fee/transparent
  pricing (Eat App, Zenchef, Servme all market "no per-cover fee" explicitly against it). One data
  point, not proof, but worth remembering next time a per-booking commission model is on the table.
- **Iran: both priority competitors have real, dated, first-party-or-independently-reported weak
  spots** — Fidilio's 2024 Snapp Food data-sync controversy (disputed "bug" explanation, three
  independent outlets) and SmartX's Aug 2025 self-admitted Customer Club outage (apology page
  bracketed by two consecutive anti-churn campaigns). Neither is speculative — see `WATCH.md` and
  the two profiles for full sourcing.

## Top 3 proposals, ranked

1. **`proposals/002-data-provenance-receipt.md`** — surface Rezervno's existing tenant-isolation
   discipline (`ctx.restaurant.id`/`auth.tenantId`-only, never body/query — already code-verified in
   `with-restaurant-auth.ts`) as a visible "this reservation is only visible to X" receipt for diners
   and an audit-trail screen for restaurant staff. Direct answer to Fidilio's 2024 controversy.
   **Gate: must be verified end-to-end by `backend-integrity-engineer` before any user-facing copy
   ships** — do not claim a guarantee we haven't actually confirmed holds everywhere.
2. **`proposals/001-single-clock-loyalty-guardrails.md`** — one expiry clock per `PointsLedger`
   entry, mandatory in-app explanation whenever any tier gets an asymmetric or changed expiry/rate,
   one forgiveness reset per year. Directly answers two dated, sourced 2026 loyalty-program
   backlashes (Starbucks' asymmetric expiry, Chipotle's stacked-clocks + litigated 180-day policy).
   **Gate: needs `data-trust-engineer` to confirm current `PointsLedger` schema before any code
   changes** — I don't know if it already has divergent expiry fields.
3. **`proposals/003-transparent-restaurant-terms.md`** — public, self-serve restaurant pricing page
   with a worked example, plus an explicit "we will never require exclusivity" commitment. Answers
   OpenTable's April 2026 lock-in clause, SmartX's "contact us" opacity, and Fidilio's undisclosed
   commission + inconsistent star-rating marketing. **Needs the founder's input** on whether a
   pricing page already exists in `apps/landing` (unverified this pass) and what Rezervno's actual
   current restaurant terms are.

## The one thing you don't know yet

**Our own competitive-research tooling has a real, reproducible gap: `WebFetch` was blocked for
every single domain tested across all three research passes this batch — including neutral control
domains like `example.com` and `en.wikipedia.org`.** This isn't a site-specific block; it's an
environment-wide restriction on this session's direct page-fetch capability. Every quote and figure
in today's four profiles came through `WebSearch`'s own server-side retrieval/synthesis, which each
profile flags explicitly and which is *why* review counts are far below the fifty-per-app target
(3 verified for Fidilio, 0 for SmartX, versus real depth on OpenTable/Resy/SevenRooms where
WebSearch's synthesis was richer). This is disclosed, not hidden, in every affected file — but if you
want the next batch to hit real review-volume targets (especially hands-on Cafe Bazaar/Myket
star-by-star review reading), Scout needs either working `WebFetch` access restored, or a different
tool path to read raw app-store review pages directly. Worth raising with whoever configures this
environment before the next research batch, not treating as a one-off.
